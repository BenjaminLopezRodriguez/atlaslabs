# Atlas Labs — Product

## One-liner

We build **specialist fine-tuned LLMs** for hard domains—with experts and consented real-world data—then extend that stack into consumer products, agent runtimes, and (slowly) the physical world.

## What we are

Atlas Labs’ center of gravity is **specialist model engineering**: fine-tunes that actually know a domain.

Everything else is how those models reach people and work:

1. **Consumer proof** — Atlas Life (kids / learning)
2. **Runtime** — software agents, workspace/CLI, Remote cloud machines
3. **Expansion** — hardware and sensing in the physical world

## What we are not (primary)

We are **not** primarily “an agentic coding workspace.” Spaces, CLI, and Remote matter as **runtime/infra** for specialists and agents. Live messaging on [atlaslabs.id](https://www.atlaslabs.id) that leads with coding spaces is delivery layer, not company identity.

## Surfaces (priority order)

```
Specialist fine-tuned LLMs
        ├── Atlas Life (consumer) → https://atlaslabs.life
        ├── Software + Remote (runtime)
        └── Hardware (physical expansion)
```

### 1. Specialist models (core)

Fine-tuned domain models co-built with experts on consented data.

Conceptual products:

- **Subsurface Map** — construction / walls / utilities
- **Field Stress** — agriculture / crop and moisture signal
- **Floor Habits** — retail buying patterns
- **Robotics Policy** — train robots from real captures
- **Kids / Learning** — powering Atlas Life (safe, age-aware)

**Job to be done:** “A model that actually knows my domain.”

### 2. Atlas Life (consumer proof)

**Domain:** [https://atlaslabs.life](https://atlaslabs.life)  
**Code (in progress):** `/Users/benji/Desktop/atlas/atlaslife/atlaslife`

A parent-held chatbot that uses Atlas specialist models so kids can learn safely—homework help, curiosity, age-appropriate talk—without open-web LLM risk.

Principles:

- Safety in how the model is asked to think, not only a filter at the end
- Parents hold the account; kids get space inside that household
- Grows with the child; optional school link
- Nourish the mind — explain before doing the homework for them

**Relationship to Labs:** Proof that our core (specialist fine-tunes) ships as a calm consumer product.

### 3. Software + Remote (runtime)

How people and agents run work against specialists:

- **Atlas Workspace**, **Job Runner**, **Capture Review**, **Atlas CLI**
- **Remote** — Agent Machine, Heavy Desk, Shared Session (dedicated cloud machines; related to minimachines acquisition / spaces story)

### 4. Hardware (slow move into the physical world)

Instruments that generate signal and run edge inference—feeds models over time, does not lead the company story:

- Wall Reader, Site Drone, Soil Rover, Crop Imager
- Uniform Sensor, Edge Node

**Program:** rent kit at a discount when customers consent to training on job data—one acquisition loop, not the whole identity.

## Company story

Atlas Labs starts from a failure mode: most AI systems reason about a world that was already easy to scrape, license, or photograph from afar. Ground-level gaps compound with every model generation.

Our founding work was about closing that gap—systems that turn real observation into structured, verifiable records for training. That lineage still sits under the company.

Today the center of gravity is **specialist fine-tuned LLMs**. Atlas Life ([atlaslabs.life](https://atlaslabs.life)) is the consumer proof. Runtime (software, Remote) and hardware are how those models operate and, slowly, meet physical work.
